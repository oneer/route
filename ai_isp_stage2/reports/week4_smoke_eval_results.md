## Notes To Fill

- Input baseline: `22.1273` PSNR / `0.28203` SSIM on `paired_rgb_smoke`.
- Visual conclusion: both models improve over the noisy input. DnCNN residual produces the stronger short-run result in this smoke setup; NAFNet-lite improves from step 40 to step 80 but still needs longer training or tuning.
- Failure cases: NAFNet-lite output is still visibly smoother and less faithful than DnCNN in this 80-step smoke run. This is expected for a newly added model with fewer steps and no tuning.
- Next step: keep DnCNN as the Week3/4 reference baseline, then train NAFNet-lite longer or compare a smaller/larger width setting before drawing model-level conclusions.
