## Analysis

- Visual conclusion: DnCNN L1/L2 are the strongest baselines under these historical
  configurations. Patch64 is close but slightly weaker globally; UNet and NAFNet-lite
  improve the noisy input but remain under-trained or less matched to this tiny setup.
- Failure cases: DnCNN still has local texture/color residual; UNet retains larger
  local pixel error; NAFNet-lite should not be judged as a full official reproduction.
- Next step: use the fixed fairness configs to separate architecture from loss and
  training-budget effects, then evaluate the frozen winner on held-out test.

The SSIM values in this historical report were generated with the earlier approximate
implementation. Use `reports/evaluation_protocol.md` and rerun evaluation before
placing them in a final benchmark table.
